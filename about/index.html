FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

STUDIO FAQ

Everything you need to know about our design process, project execution, materials, timelines, and working with Kalpravah.

Our process begins with understanding how you live. We map your home’s sunlight, ventilation, spatial constraints, and everyday habits before developing 3D renders, layouts, design concepts, detailed drawings, and material selections. Once the design is finalised, we coordinate execution to bring the vision to life.

We combine online consultations, digital presentations, detailed drawings, and regular progress updates to keep projects connected from anywhere in India. Site coordination and execution arrangements are planned according to each project’s location and scope.

We strictly specify zero-emission E0 grade timber, marine-grade boiling water proof (BWP) plywood, German Blum/Hettich hardware fittings, and natural stone veneers for lasting interior luxury.

You can submit an inquiry through our online Concierge Desk or connect directly with our Principal Architect via WhatsApp for an immediate consultation.
